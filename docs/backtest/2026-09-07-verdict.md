# Backtest audit — 2026-09-07

**Setup.** `python3 -m trading_engine.core.backtester --seeds 100` (365 daily cycles after a 200-bar warm-up,
4 regimes × 100 seeds = 400 runs per variant). Cost model: 40 bps taker + 10 bps slippage per side, zero spread
(100 bps round trip). Gates: 10% cap ($10), 30% gross, 3% daily kill-switch, fee-vs-edge, Kraken order minimums,
90/10 sweep. The harness runs the production `Daemon → Strategy → RiskManager → MockBroker → Ledger` stack.

**Harness bug found and fixed first.** The initial run capped every 365-day simulation at ~7 fills because the
daemon treated the `RegimeFeed` subclass as a live feed and never rolled the trading day. Fixed in `38d61f0`
(`isinstance(SyntheticFeed)`), regression-tested, and the grid was re-run. The tables below are from the fixed run.

## Verdict

1. **The current RSI/trend-pullback strategy is not net positive after 40 bps fees.** Pooled across regimes it
   loses $1.20 per year on $100 (median -$3.22, profit factor 0.92) and is profitable in only 40% of runs.
   It makes money in exactly one regime (bull: +$13.55, PF 2.07) and loses in the other three.
2. **Fees are a secondary cost.** Round-trip cost is ~$1.75 per year at 58 fills; the chop regime alone loses
   $9.85. Reducing turnover helps (slow_trend pays $1.19/yr) but does not fix a negative gross edge outside
   trends.
3. **Timeframe adjustments help but do not make it regime-neutral.** Longer lookbacks (SMA 30/100, 60-day
   momentum), holding winners (RSI exit 90, 3× ATR) and a wider entry band turn the pooled mean positive
   (+$2.36, PF 1.06) by capturing more of the bull. The median run is still negative and bear/chop/black-swan
   still lose. Loosening further (`wide_band`) buys bull upside at the cost of a -47% worst drawdown and the
   kill-switch firing in half of all runs; reject it.
4. **Capital survival is not in question; compute self-funding is.** No run went below $54 and no variant ever
   reached $0 (no leverage). But the 10% reserve yields **$0.66–0.78 per year** while a weekly Claude review
   costs ~$0.18 × 52 ≈ $9.40 per year. At a $100 base the strategy cannot fund its own compute; the $100 credit
   pool simply lasts ~10 years at weekly cadence (longer at monthly).

**Required before live deployment.**
- Adopt the `slow_trend` parameters (all inside `STRATEGY_PARAM_BOUNDS`): `fast_sma=30, slow_sma=100,
  momentum_lookback=60, rsi_exit=90, rsi_entry_max=75, atr_stop_mult=3.0, edge_capture=0.35`.
- Add a **regime filter** so the system sits out bear and chop: e.g. trade only when the equal-weight basket is
  above its 100-day SMA *and* 60-day momentum exceeds a floor (the chop PF of 0.43–0.50 across every variant
  is the single largest leak), and suspend entries while the realized-vol z-score is elevated after a shock.
- Re-run this grid after the filter; the target is a pooled median > 0 and chop PF ≥ 1.0.
- Cut the bridge cadence to monthly; weekly reviews cost 12× what the reserve earns.
